LORE ENCYCLOPEDIA

⚡ The Aura Stream

Melting Point College sits on an ancient spiritual nexus in Port Harcourt. Students who push themselves academically and physically can tap into raw aura energy. Higher aura = better focus, stronger presence, and sometimes... strange powers.

👁️ Isi Owu - The WAEC Guardian

Once a brilliant teacher, now corrupted by the school's energy. He believes only the strongest students deserve to pass. In Chapter 7 he becomes the final boss.

🏫 Melting Point College

Founded decades ago. Rumours say the original builders sealed something under the basement. Every year a few students "awaken" more than others.

👑 Aura King

The legendary title given to the student with the highest aura rank. Only a handful have ever reached it. The current generation is close...
